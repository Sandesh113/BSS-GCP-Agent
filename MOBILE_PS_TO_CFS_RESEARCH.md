# UK mobile Product Specification to CFS research

Research date: 21 October 2026. Scope: the ten candidate commercial Product Specifications derived from EE, Lyca Mobile, Lebara, and Vodafone SIM-only offers. **The CFS names and mappings below are a proposed reference design, not a claim that any of those operators publishes this internal catalogue.** Public operator pages expose the customer offer; TM Forum and vendor material explains the catalog and fulfillment pattern.

## Modelling rules supported by the sources

- [TM Forum SID Figure SO.08](https://www.tmforum.org/Browsable_HTML_SID_R20.0/content/_3E3F0EC000E93ED67838027E-content.html) defines `ProductSpecRealizedAsCFSSpec`; a CFS specification may be used by multiple products and requires supporting RFS specifications. The instance-level association is `ProductRealizedAsCFService`.
- The [TM Forum IG1228 mobile discussion](https://engage.tmforum.org/communities/community-home/digestviewer/viewthread?CommunityKey=d543b8ba-9d3a-4121-85ce-5b68e6c31ce5&GroupId=31&MessageKey=a85e243f-0355-4873-a1ee-fde4ba954400&hlmlt=VT&tab=digestviewer) identifies **Mobile Line, National Voice Bucket, SMS Bucket, and Data Bucket** as separate CFSs and notes the buckets' dependency on Mobile Line. It is an example, not a mandatory universal taxonomy.
- [IG1233](https://www.tmforum.org/resources/reference/ig1233-product-service-modelling-best-practices-conforming-to-oda/) proposes keeping product and service models decoupled. A [TM Forum chief architect's explanation](https://engage.tmforum.org/communities/community-home/digestviewer/viewthread?CommunityKey=d543b8ba-9d3a-4121-85ce-5b68e6c31ce5&GroupId=31&MessageKey=ccdd72ba-b73c-4106-ae95-f2a36d4e8c36&hlmlt=VT&tab=digestviewer) describes a best-practice restriction of a product spec to a single CFS, while explicitly saying the APIs do not impose that restriction. Use a composite CFS if an atomic mapping needs several underlying CFSs.
- [Salesforce Communications Cloud](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/es-MX/content/learn/modules/industries-product-modeling-basics/examine-industry-specific-product-models) describes commercial Product Specs, CFSS, RFSS, and resources; [Salesforce's EPC material](https://developer.salesforce.com/files/credential-resources/CME_EPC_PDF-en.pdf) shows that multiple commercial products can use a CFSS. [Hansen](https://inform.tmforum.org/features-and-opinion/my-api-story-shaping-our-business-and-driving-innovation) describes a shared catalogue exposed through TMF620/633/641; [Netcracker](https://www.netcracker.com/portfolio/solutions/monetization-and-customer-experience/intelligent-sales-automation) describes catalog-driven decomposition into services/resources. An [Amdocs-hosted industry study](https://www.amdocs.com/sites/default/files/2024-10/catalog-management-telco-it-and-the-core-of-customer-engagement-14-10-2024.pdf) describes product, CFS and resource catalog layers. None publishes the ten exact PS-to-CFS mappings below.

## Proposed reference CFS set

For an initial service catalogue: **Mobile Line**, **Mobile Voice with voice buckets**, **Mobile SMS with SMS bucket**, **Mobile Data with data bucket**, **Voicemail**, **MMS**, and **Shared Allowance / Multi-SIM**. Represent **International Calling** as a voice allowance/rating policy, **Roaming** as a Mobile Line entitlement plus usage policies, and both **Smartwatch Connectivity** and **Data Gifting** through the shared allowance CFS. Thus ten commercial specifications map to **seven** reusable CFS types in this baseline, not ten. A separate roaming CFS remains an extension if an operator manages it independently.

### Cardinality and dependency rules

| CFS | Cardinality per mobile subscription order | Dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile Line | `1..n` (one primary is mandatory; additional instances only for companion/secondary identities when required) | None |
| Mobile Voice | `0..1` per line | Mobile Line |
| Mobile SMS | `0..1` per line | Mobile Line |
| Mobile Data | `0..1` per line | Mobile Line |
| Voicemail | `0..1` per voice-enabled line | Mobile Voice |
| MMS | `0..1` per eligible line | Mobile Line plus required messaging/data capabilities |
| Shared Allowance / Multi-SIM | `0..1` per sharing group, with `0..n` member lines/devices | Primary Mobile Line; Mobile Data for data gifts; eligible Voice/SMS/Data for companion sharing |

These are proposed catalogue constraints, not TM Forum-prescribed cardinalities. The `1..n` Mobile Line count is deliberate: the order must contain at least one primary line, while a watch may require its own network identity. A data gift between two existing lines amends balances without creating a line. Duplicate PS mappings to the same CFS must be merged into one service instance/configuration. A commercial offering can still require a particular optional CFS even though the generic model marks it `0..1`.

**Identifier distinction:** an IMSI (or, in 5G, an IMSI-form SUPI) identifies a subscription; the MSISDN is a phone number. A shared-number watch can have a second IMSI while presenting the primary phone number. Data gifting normally links two pre-existing subscriptions and moves a balance, with no new IMSI. [Ericsson/Turkcell deployment](https://www.ericsson.com/en/press-releases/5/2024/ericsson-and-turkcell-bring-seamless-esim-activation-to-smartwatches-in-turkiye), [Ericsson on SUPI/IMSI](https://www.ericsson.com/en/blog/2020/1/5g-security-sim-card).

The commercial UI should show the ten Product Specifications and their characteristics. Service designers maintain CFS mapping, dependencies, characteristic transforms, RFS/activation integrations and ownership separately. A product order should resolve the unique CFS instances and merge amendments to a shared CFS before sending service orders, rather than creating one duplicate service per commercial component.

## Open modelling decisions before implementation

1. Whether an allowance bucket is a distinct CFS instance or a characteristic of Mobile Voice/SMS/Data. IG1228 uses distinct bucket CFSs; operational ownership and independent change/assurance should decide the final granularity.
2. Whether a particular operator manages roaming independently. Baseline: reuse Mobile Line; a separate roaming CFS is an extension for independent fulfillment/assurance.
3. Whether voicemail and MMS are selectable products or included service features. If always included, retain their service realization but do not force separate customer-visible sellable offers.
4. Which 4G/5G network realization to simulate. The catalogue should not hard-code HSS as a universal 5G target; [Ericsson](https://www.ericsson.com/en/core-network/5g-core/subscriber-data-management) distinguishes HSS (4G/IMS) from 5G subscription management. A 5G roaming change may involve UDM/UDR, AMF and PCF policies as well as charging, not a single HSS flag.
